- Apply through SATAC. Check its key dates now so you don't miss early rounds.

### Study and training after school (1 to 4 years after school)

- Nursing Support / Personal Care Worker: Certificate III in Health Services Assistance or Individual Support. Another route: A traineeship in health services or individual support.
