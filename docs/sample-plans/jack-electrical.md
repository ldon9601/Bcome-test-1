### Finish school strong (Years 11 and 12)

- Go to a trades expo or TAFE open day and talk to apprentices about their first year.
- Get some real-world experience: a part-time job, volunteering or a short course linked to air conditioning and refrigeration mechanic.
- Ask your school about a school-based apprenticeship or traineeship.

### Study and training after school (1 to 4 years after school)

- Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanic: Certificate III in Refrigeration and Air Conditioning (apprenticeship).
- Electrician: Certificate III in Electrotechnology Electrician (4-year apprenticeship).
- Carpenter / Joiner: Certificate III in Carpentry (apprenticeship).
- Look for employers or group training organisations that support apprentices through to their licence.
- Ask which local employers take on apprentices; your school and TAFE often know.
