### Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Technician (OSCA 382131)

Personal fit 84 · Future opportunity 66 · Pathway feasibility 95

It suits your interests and values well, has a steady job outlook and the way in fits your plans.

**Outlook:** Estimated growth of about 9% by May 2035, based on JSA projections for similar jobs (all jobs: 13.3%).

**How to get there:** Refrigeration and air conditioning apprenticeship (Certificate III), then your licence (4 years).

### Finish school strong (Years 11 and 12)

- Go to a trades expo or TAFE open day and talk to apprentices about their first year.
- Get some real-world experience: a part-time job, volunteering or a short course linked to air conditioning and refrigeration technician.
- Ask your school about a school-based apprenticeship or traineeship.

### Study and training after school (About 3–4 years after school, depending on the career)

- Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Technician: Refrigeration and air conditioning apprenticeship (Certificate III), then your licence (4 years).
- Electrician: Electrical apprenticeship (Certificate III in Electrotechnology Electrician), then your licence (4 years).
- Automotive Technician (Mechanic): Automotive apprenticeship (Certificate III in Light Vehicle Mechanical Technology) (3–4 years).
- Look for employers or group training organisations that support apprentices through to their licence.
- Ask which local employers take on apprentices; your school and TAFE often know.
