### Health and Welfare Services Manager (OSCA 1413)

Personal fit 81 · Future opportunity 100 · Pathway feasibility 88

It suits your interests and values well, has a strong job outlook and the way in fits your plans.

**Outlook:** Jobs and Skills Australia projects employment to grow 27.1% by May 2035, compared with 13.3% for all jobs.

**How to get there:** Bachelor of Health Services Management, or a clinical degree, then several years' experience (8–12 years).

**Time to qualify:** 8–12 years after Year 12 (about 10–14 years from now) to reach: Managing a health or welfare service

**Pay (indicative):** $110k–$130k starting, $130k–$180k experienced. Usually reached after a clinical or care career.

**Skills:** Leading and managing people, Budgeting and cost control, Policy and regulation, Project management. Core: Planning and organising, Oral communication, Problem solving

- University: Bachelor of Health Services Management (AQF 7), builds Leading and managing people, Budgeting and cost control, Policy and regulation

**Skills also lead to:** Construction Project Manager (Project management, Budgeting and cost control, Leading and managing people); ICT Manager (Leading and managing people, Project management, Budgeting and cost control); Research and Development Manager (Leading and managing people, Project management, Budgeting and cost control)
