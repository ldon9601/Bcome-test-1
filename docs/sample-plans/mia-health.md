## Plan

Your strongest interests are Social, Investigative and Conventional. Careers like registered nurse and physiotherapist fit those interests and have a positive job outlook.

### Registered Nurse

Matches your top interests: Social, Investigative and Conventional. Offers what you value: helping people, a stable, secure job and making a difference. Builds on subjects you enjoy or do well in: Biology, Chemistry, PDHPE / Health and Movement and English.

**Outlook:** Estimated growth of about 26% by May 2035, based on JSA projections for similar jobs (all jobs: 13.3%).

**How to get there:** Bachelor of Nursing. Another route: Diploma of Nursing (Enrolled Nurse), then a bachelor's with credit.

### Physiotherapist

Matches your top interests: Social and Investigative. Offers what you value: helping people and making a difference. Builds on subjects you enjoy or do well in: Biology and PDHPE / Health and Movement.

**Outlook:** Jobs and Skills Australia projects employment to grow 33.6% by May 2035, compared with 13.3% for all jobs.

**How to get there:** Bachelor of Physiotherapy, or a related bachelor's then Doctor of Physiotherapy.

### Dietitian

Matches your top interests: Investigative and Social. Offers what you value: helping people and making a difference. Builds on subjects you enjoy or do well in: Biology, Chemistry and PDHPE / Health and Movement.

**Outlook:** Estimated growth of about 26% by May 2035, based on JSA projections for similar jobs (all jobs: 13.3%).

**How to get there:** Bachelor of Nutrition and Dietetics.

### Finish school strong (Years 10 to 12)

- Subjects that build useful skills: Biology, Chemistry, PDHPE / Health and Movement, English.
- Ask your careers adviser about work experience related to registered nurse.

### Study and training after school (1 to 4 years after school)

- Registered Nurse: Bachelor of Nursing. Another route: Diploma of Nursing (Enrolled Nurse), then a bachelor's with credit.
- Physiotherapist: Bachelor of Physiotherapy, or a related bachelor's then Doctor of Physiotherapy.
- Dietitian: Bachelor of Nutrition and Dietetics.
- Choose courses with work placements or industry projects; they lead to first jobs.

### Your first years at work (First 3 years of work)

- Complete any registration, licence or accreditation your job needs.
- Keep building the skills employers want most: patient care, clinical assessment, communication, teamwork under pressure.
- Learn to use the AI tools in your field well; they will be part of most jobs.
- Find a mentor at work and review this plan every year.

### Future of work

- Most of the work is hands-on or person-to-person, which AI is unlikely to replace.
