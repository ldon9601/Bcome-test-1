## Plan

Your strongest interests are Social, Investigative and Conventional. Careers like registered nurse and physiotherapist fit those interests and have a positive job outlook.

### Registered Nurse (OSCA 2654)

Personal fit 85 · Future opportunity 91 · Pathway feasibility 95

It suits your interests and values well, has a strong job outlook and the way in fits your plans.

**Outlook:** Estimated growth of about 26% by May 2035, based on JSA projections for similar jobs (all jobs: 13.3%).

**How to get there:** Bachelor of Nursing, then register with AHPRA (3 years). Another route: Diploma of Nursing (enrolled nurse), then a nursing degree with credit.

**Time to qualify:** 3–4 years after Year 12 (about 5–6 years from now) to reach: Registered nurse

**Pay (indicative):** $75k–$85k starting, $95k–$120k experienced

**Skills:** Person-centred care, Clinical assessment, Medicines management, Infection control, Anatomy and physiology, Emergency care. Core: Oral communication, Teamwork, Problem solving

- University: Bachelor of Nursing (AQF 7), builds Person-centred care, Clinical assessment, Medicines management, Infection control, Anatomy and physiology
- TAFE or VET: Diploma of Nursing (AQF 5), builds Person-centred care, Clinical assessment, Medicines management, Infection control, Anatomy and physiology

**Skills also lead to:** General Practitioner (Doctor) (Clinical assessment, Anatomy and physiology, Medicines management, Person-centred care); Assistant in Nursing (Person-centred care, Infection control, Anatomy and physiology); Paramedic (Emergency care, Clinical assessment, Medicines management)

### Physiotherapist (OSCA 262431)

Personal fit 76 · Future opportunity 100 · Pathway feasibility 89

It suits some of your interests, has a strong job outlook and the way in fits your plans.

**Outlook:** Jobs and Skills Australia projects employment to grow 33.6% by May 2035, compared with 13.3% for all jobs.

**How to get there:** Bachelor of Physiotherapy, or a related degree then a Doctor of Physiotherapy (4–6 years).

**Time to qualify:** 4–6 years after Year 12 (about 6–8 years from now) to reach: Registered physiotherapist

**Pay (indicative):** $75k–$85k starting, $100k–$130k experienced

**Skills:** Anatomy and physiology, Clinical assessment, Rehabilitation and therapy planning, Coaching and motivating. Core: Oral communication, Problem solving, Learning

- University: Bachelor of Physiotherapy (or Doctor of Physiotherapy) (AQF 7), builds Anatomy and physiology, Clinical assessment, Rehabilitation and therapy planning

**Skills also lead to:** Occupational Therapist (Rehabilitation and therapy planning, Clinical assessment); Speech Pathologist (Rehabilitation and therapy planning, Clinical assessment); Fitness Instructor (Coaching and motivating, Anatomy and physiology)
