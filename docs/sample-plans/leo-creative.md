## Plan

Your strongest interests are Artistic, Enterprising and Social. Careers like marketing professional and actor / dancer / entertainer fit those interests and have a positive job outlook.

### Marketing Professional

Matches your top interests: Enterprising and Artistic. Offers what you value: being creative and variety. Builds on subjects you enjoy or do well in: Business Studies, Media / Film and Visual Arts.

**Outlook:** Estimated growth of about 24% by May 2035, based on JSA projections for similar jobs (all jobs: 13.3%).

**How to get there:** Bachelor of Business (Marketing) or Communications. Another route: Diploma of Marketing and Communication.

### Actor / Dancer / Entertainer

Matches your top interests: Artistic, Enterprising and Social. Offers what you value: being creative, variety and working your own way.

**Outlook:** Estimated growth of about 20% by May 2035, based on JSA projections for similar jobs (all jobs: 13.3%).

**How to get there:** Bachelor of Fine Arts (Acting) or Dance. Another route: Diploma of Musical Theatre or Dance.

### Graphic / Web Designer

Matches your top interests: Artistic and Enterprising. Offers what you value: being creative, working your own way and variety. Builds on subjects you enjoy or do well in: Visual Arts and Media / Film.

**Outlook:** Estimated growth of about 24% by May 2035, based on JSA projections for similar jobs (all jobs: 13.3%).

**How to get there:** Bachelor of Design (Visual Communication). Another route: Diploma of Graphic Design.

### Finish school strong (Years 11 and 12)

- Go to at least two university or TAFE open days and talk to current students.
- Get some real-world experience: a part-time job, volunteering or a short course linked to marketing professional.

### Study and training after school (1 to 4 years after school)

- Marketing Professional: Bachelor of Business (Marketing) or Communications. Another route: Diploma of Marketing and Communication.
- Actor / Dancer / Entertainer: Bachelor of Fine Arts (Acting) or Dance. Another route: Diploma of Musical Theatre or Dance.
- Graphic / Web Designer: Bachelor of Design (Visual Communication). Another route: Diploma of Graphic Design.
- Choose courses with work placements or industry projects; they lead to first jobs.

### Your first years at work (First 3 years of work)

- Complete any registration, licence or accreditation your job needs.
- Keep building the skills employers want most: market research, content and campaigns, digital analytics, communication.
- Learn to use the AI tools in your field well; they will be part of most jobs.
- Find a mentor at work and review this plan every year.

### Future of work

- Over 90% of employment growth to May 2035 is in jobs that usually need a post-school qualification (skill levels 1 to 4).
- AI and automation are likely to change much of the routine work, so the role will reward people who build specialist and people skills.
- Most of the work is hands-on or person-to-person, which AI is unlikely to replace.
